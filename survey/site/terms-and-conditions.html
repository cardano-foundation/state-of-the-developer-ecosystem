Privacy Policy

This survey is organised by Cardano Stiftung (Cardano Foundation), which is responsible for the processing of personal data in connection with the survey. Please read carefully before publishing your answers.

What we collect

We collect the answers you submit and process certain technical information necessary to operate the survey, as described below. We do not ask for your name or email address. Until you submit them, your answers stay in your browser.

What is public

Your answers, and when they were published, are public on the Cardano blockchain. Although we do not ask you to identify yourself, your answers and associated blockchain information may allow you to be identified. For example, a text answer, such as a project link, or your answers taken together can be enough to recognise you. If you pay the transaction fee with your own wallet, your response is also linked to that wallet.

Given the public and permanent nature of blockchain records, please carefully consider the information you include in your responses.

What our server keeps

Our server keeps a record of the responses it helps publish, which are public anyway. Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address, to operate and secure the survey, and keeps our server's logs for a few days. Before publishing, Cloudflare Turnstile reads signals from your browser to check that you are not a robot. We use no analytics.

How your data is used

When the survey closes, the responses are read from the blockchain and processed into a public report, like those of 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025. The dataset and the code that builds it are public on GitHub.

To the extent that personal data is processed in connection with the survey, we do so in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where the GDPR applies, rely on our legitimate interests in conducting the survey and publishing its results under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
